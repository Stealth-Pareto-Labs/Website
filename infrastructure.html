Infrastructure

The AI, and the machines it runs on.

Pareto runs sovereign inference and cloud for emerging markets. Open-source models, optimized and served on infrastructure we operate — inside your own country, under your own law. Anyone can use it: one developer with an API key, a startup, a team, a ministry.

Get access See both engines

A developer in São Paulo, Jakarta or Lagos should not have to put their country’s data in a region on another continent. That is the whole point.

Why we build it

Models commoditize. The machines don’t.

Open-weight models now match closed ones for most real work, and they keep getting cheaper to run. The scarce thing is no longer the model. It is who adapts it, who serves it, and whose jurisdiction it answers in.

01

The model is no longer the moat

Open-source weights are a commodity input. Value moves to whoever tunes them to a domain, serves them fast, and keeps them running cheaply.

02

Inference is the recurring cost

Training happens once. Inference happens on every request, every day, forever. That is where the spend concentrates, and where it compounds.

03

Emerging markets rent all of it

Teams across Africa, Latin America, South and Southeast Asia build on compute they do not own, in regions they cannot reach, billed in a currency they do not earn.

04

Data has a jurisdiction

Where a workload runs decides whose law reaches it. For a hospital, a bank or a ministry that is not a preference. It is the requirement.
